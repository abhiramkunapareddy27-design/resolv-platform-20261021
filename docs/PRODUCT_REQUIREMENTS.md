# Product Requirements

ReSolv — real problems, real talent, real outcomes.

One account connects problem publishing, participation, collaboration and evidence. Company, student and freelancer roles; administrators are provisioned outside public signup. Public records are published problems only. No simulated payment, verification, customers or usage metrics. Local example content is explicitly identified.

MVP: authentication, problem drafts/publication, search, saved opportunities, applications, teams, hackathon registration/submissions, profiles, organization moderation and contract foundations. Advanced judging, disputes and billing require further implementation and review.

## Recording analysis

Primary reference: user-provided 34.73-second screen recording, inspected through 16 extracted keyframes and enlarged frames at 4.3, 8.7 and 17.4 seconds. A contact sheet exists in /tmp/resolv-reference-frames/contact-sheet.png for this session.

0–4 seconds: a substantial structure is grounded in a desaturated environment; camera orbits and approaches. 4–9 seconds: upper blocks detach and turn individually; the original structure stays recognizable. 6–16 seconds: an interior/gallery state appears with sparse annotations. 17–20 seconds: concentric forms frame the camera and lead through depth. 23–28 seconds: the viewpoint returns to the exterior and pieces reform. The latter frames demonstrate reversal and partial reopening. Small corner typography and a consistent atmosphere keep these states in one world. The recording does not reveal the source implementation; camera/scroll relationships are inferred from visible motion.

Adopt continuous camera travel, coordinated separation, deep framing, restrained labels and reversible assembly. Do not copy the igloo, mountain environment, penguin artwork, branding or luminous materials.

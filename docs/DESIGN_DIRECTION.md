# Design direction

## The Common Form

A quiet, atmospheric entrance around an original physical sculpture: three bands of chamfered mineral segments. They begin as a layered vessel, separate to reveal different perspectives, and reconnect in three intersecting planes. Sage, forest, chalk and one terracotta segment create a recognizable material language without borrowed models or stock assets.

The user-supplied IGLOO recording is the primary motion reference. Adopt its continuous world, camera travel, coordinated deconstruction, spatial passage and reversal. Do not copy its igloo, mountains, artwork, branding or luminous materials. Detailed observations and keyframes are recorded in the redesign audit and storyboard.

Porcelain, parchment, forest, sage, terracotta and warm stone are semantic tokens. The cinematic environment uses a desaturated variation; dark mode uses ink-green surfaces and intentionally relit materials. Instrument Serif provides selective editorial emphasis; Manrope provides interface clarity; IBM Plex Mono provides small metadata. All three fonts are self-hosted and their Fontsource packages declare OFL-1.1.

## Page identities

- Home: one bounded cinematic journey with minimal peripheral typography and direct entry choices.
- Explore: information-rich ruled discovery list, search and comparison.
- Hackathons: an ink-green arena masthead, competition count and deadline-focused briefs.
- Freelance: a professional project ledger emphasizing disclosed budget, scope and proposal entry.
- Collaborate: open team profiles, skills and membership requests.
- Talent: editorial people directory with shared projects and confirmed outcomes kept distinct.
- Portfolio: calm settings/work management, plus a separate public body of work.
- Dashboard: compact real-record summaries and focused workflow tabs.
- Post a problem: a focused three-step brief/review flow with restrained guidance.
- About: one short editorial statement rather than a second feature catalogue.

The earlier stacked marketing narrative and wireframe network were removed. Shared forms, dialogs and navigation use consistent tokens; dedicated product pages retain their own compositions. The prior reference study of Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Framer and Awwwards informed hierarchy and restraint; the supplied video governs the redesigned cinematic behavior.
